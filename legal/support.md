# StashtagAI Support

Need a hand with StashtagAI? We're here to help. Our support team can help with questions about your account, saving and organizing Instagram and TikTok links, AI-extracted details that don't look right, bugs or unexpected behavior, and privacy or data deletion requests.

## Contact us

Email: **[support@stashtag.ai](mailto:support@stashtag.ai)**

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

- The email address associated with your StashtagAI account
- Your device and app version (for example, iPhone 15, iOS 18, StashtagAI 1.2)
- A short description of the problem and, if possible, the link or screenshot involved

We aim to respond within 2 business days.
