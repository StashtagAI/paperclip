# StashtagAI Support

Need a hand with StashtagAI? We're here to help. Our support team can help with questions about your account, saving and organizing Instagram and TikTok links, AI-extracted details that don't look right, shared lists, location and nearby nudges, bugs or unexpected behavior, and privacy or data deletion requests.

## Contact us

Email: **[support@stashtag.ai](mailto:support@stashtag.ai)**

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

- The email address associated with your StashtagAI account
- Your device and app version (for example, iPhone 15, iOS 18, StashtagAI 1.2)
- A short description of the problem and, if possible, the link or screenshot involved

We aim to respond within 2 business days.

## Help in the app

You can also get help without leaving StashtagAI. Tap the **profile** button (the person icon at the top of the home screen), then:

- **Send feedback** to report a bug or share an idea. Your app version and platform are included automatically.
- **Contact support** to email us. Your app version and platform are filled in for you.
- **Sign out everywhere** if you think someone else has access to your account.
- **Delete account** to permanently delete your account and data. See our [Data Deletion Policy](data-deletion.md) for details.

If an item in one of your lists is wrong, tap **Wrong?** next to it to tell us what's wrong. You can also give a saved post a thumbs up or down.
