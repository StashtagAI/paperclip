## 1. What the Service does

StashtagAI lets you save links to public Instagram and TikTok posts, including by sharing them to StashtagAI from another app. We retrieve publicly available content from those posts (such as the caption, author name, and, where available, a transcript of the audio or the text in the video), and may read public websites the post links to. We use automated tools, including artificial intelligence, to pull out recommendations such as places to eat, travel spots, recipes, products to shop for, things to watch or read, and things to do, and organize them into lists in your account (for example, "Eats > Denver"). For places, we may add details from Google Maps, such as ratings and opening hours.

If we can't read a post, you can paste its caption or pick screenshots or a screen recording of it. The app reads the text on your device and sends us only that text.

You can also add your own notes, ratings, and photos to items, mark items as done, share lists with other StashtagAI users, and, if you allow location access, see saved places near you and get optional nearby reminders ("nudges").
